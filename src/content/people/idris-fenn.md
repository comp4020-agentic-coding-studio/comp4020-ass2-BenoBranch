Came to teaching from a support-engineering background --- five years
reading incident postmortems for a living, which turns out to be most of
what auditing a deprecation notice actually is: find the claim, find the
evidence, find the gap between them.

Runs weeks 2, 4, 6, 8, 9, 11 and 12, with a particular interest in the weeks
where the pitch's numbers are true and the argument against the migration
has to be made anyway.

## Getting help

Sessions are the fastest way to get a question answered --- bring it to the
one right after the notice it's about, while the specifics are still in
your head. Email works for anything session timing doesn't fit, and
usually gets a reply within two working days. If a question is really about
the course as a whole rather than one week's marking, it's worth taking to
Marisol instead.
