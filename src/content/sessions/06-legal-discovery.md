Discovery review is a genuine efficiency case and a genuine training-ground
case at the same time, and this week's notice is the first to hold both
without resolving them into a single verdict. That's the harder audit skill:
not picking a side, but stating clearly what's true on each one.

## Before the session

Bring your own one-sentence version of why a task can be worth automating
and worth keeping, for different reasons, without contradiction.

## In the session

We work through this week's notice against the `spec` above, then spend the
second half planning: File Your Own Notice is due the Monday after the
break, and this session is the last structured audit practice before you
write one unsupervised.

## Afterwards

Bring an industry idea for your own notice to next session, even a rough
one --- the break is a good time to notice a pitch you've heard in the wild.
