One session before the capstone, and this week's audit skill is one you'll
need for it directly: checking a pitch's opening claim against the
migration path's actual, narrower scope. It's the same move as week 3's
framing audit, sharpened.

## Before the session

Bring the exact word in this week's pitch ("harvesting") and the exact
qualifier in the migration path ("soft fruit," "favourable rows") that
narrows it.

## In the session

Fast round: everyone finds the scope-gap in a notice from a different week
than the one they prepared, to check the skill transfers rather than just
being memorized for this one case.

## Afterwards

Bring this scope-gap check into your Sunset Review Board preparation --- it's
one of the fastest ways to test whether a pitch you're defending or blocking
is overselling its own reach.
