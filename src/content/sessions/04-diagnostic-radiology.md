Every audit so far has had at least one number in the pitch worth
challenging. This week doesn't. The published accuracy figures are real, so
the audit skill under practice is different: arguing against a migration
whose immediate case is true, on the strength of what it costs a profession
over a decade.

## Before the session

Bring one reason the pitch's accuracy numbers, even if fully accurate, don't
settle the question this notice is actually about.

## In the session

Debate format: half the room argues the pitch, half argues the audit, using
only the text of this week's notice. Notice which side has to reach further
than the page in front of them.

## Afterwards

This is the week to update your Weekly Audit Log with a note on what a
"true but insufficient" pitch looks like --- you'll need that distinction
for weeks where the pitch is far less true.
