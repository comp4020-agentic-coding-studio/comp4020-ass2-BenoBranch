Every objection so far has either disputed a number in the pitch or added
context the pitch left out. This week's economic breaking-changes section
does neither --- it accepts the pitch's numbers and argues they were never
the right numbers to look at. That's a harder move to make well, and worth
being able to name precisely.

## Before the session

Bring one number from this week's pitch, and one sentence on what question
it actually answers versus what question the reader assumes it answers.

## In the session

Paired close-reading: swap the "measuring the wrong thing" argument onto an
earlier week's notice and see whether it applies there too, or whether
something about care work specifically makes it fit here and not elsewhere.

## Afterwards

Your Weekly Audit Log entry this week should include one industry, not from
this course, where you suspect the same measuring-the-wrong-thing move is
happening.
