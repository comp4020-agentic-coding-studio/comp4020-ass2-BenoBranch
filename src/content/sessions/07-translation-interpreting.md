This week's rollback plan makes a move worth naming: "the high-stakes tier
still requires a human" sounds like safety, but the notice itself argues
that tier depends on a practice pipeline the routine-tier automation is
quietly cutting off. The audit skill is catching a rollback plan's
reassurance and checking it against the rest of the same document.

## Before the session

Bring the sentence in this week's rollback plan that sounds most reassuring,
and check it against the breaking-changes section directly.

## In the session

File Your Own Notice is due today. We spend the session in peer-audit pairs:
trade notices, and apply this week's "check the reassurance against the
rest of the document" habit to each other's work before submission.

## Afterwards

Keep your peer's audit notes --- the Sunset Review Board in week 12 revisits
this kind of peer-checked claim.
