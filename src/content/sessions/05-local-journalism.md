This week's notice argues that automating routine coverage doesn't leave a
smaller newsroom behind, it leaves a different one. That's a subtler claim
than earlier weeks' straightforward cost-and-capability arguments, and it's
worth being able to state plainly rather than just gesturing at.

## Before the session

Bring one kind of story a beat reporter produces that the pitch's own
description of the job --- "assembling public information into copy" ---
would never lead you to expect.

## In the session

Small groups draft one sentence each stating what changes about a
newsroom's relationship to its community, not just its output volume. Share
and compare.

## Afterwards

Note in your Weekly Audit Log where you'd look for evidence either way ---
what would confirm or disconfirm "the institution changed kind" for a real
newsroom you could actually check.
