Week 1 practised the audit skill on a case with a known outcome. This week's
notice --- call-centre automation --- is still in progress, so there's no
answer key. The habit is the same: read for the seam, check whether the
parts agree with each other, and don't let confident prose stand in for
evidence either way.

## Before the session

Read this week's notice once for the pitch, once for the audit, and bring
one sentence from each you're not sure you'd trust without more evidence.

## In the session

Short demos of what each pair found, then group discussion on where "we
don't know yet" is the honest answer versus where the notice already gives
you enough to judge.

## Afterwards

Your Weekly Audit Log entry for this week is the first one that isn't
practising on a resolved case. Say what you'd still need to know to be
confident either way, not just what you concluded.
