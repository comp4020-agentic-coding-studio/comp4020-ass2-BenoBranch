The pitch calls long-haul driving a control-loop task --- hold a lane, hold
a speed. That framing is doing work before the breaking-changes section ever
gets a say: it already defines the job as the part a machine is good at. The
audit skill this week is noticing a framing move before you get to the
evidence that contradicts it.

## Before the session

Bring one sentence from the pitch where the industry's task is defined
narrowly enough that the "efficient" comparison becomes easy to win.

## In the session

Group read-through of the pitch's opening framing, then a check against the
functional breaking-changes section: does it describe a part of the job the
pitch's framing already excluded?

## Afterwards

Log this week's framing move in your Weekly Audit Log --- it's a pattern
worth watching for in every notice from here on, including the one you'll
write yourself.
