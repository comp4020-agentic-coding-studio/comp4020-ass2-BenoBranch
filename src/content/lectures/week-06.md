industry: Legal discovery / paralegal review
pitch: >-
  Document review in discovery is reading thousands of pages to find the
  hundred that matter, tagged for relevance and privilege. A model trained on
  the matter's own key terms reviews the entire document set overnight, flags
  every plausibly relevant document with a defensible audit trail, and never
  needs a second associate brought on to hit the production deadline.
migrationPath: >-
  The full document set is ingested and scored for relevance and privilege
  against matter-specific criteria; a much smaller supervising team reviews
  only the model's flagged set and any documents it scored as uncertain.
breakingChanges:
  economic: >-
    First- and second-year associate work has long included discovery review
    specifically because it's how junior lawyers learn a practice area's
    documents, vocabulary, and fact patterns before they're trusted with
    judgment calls. Automating the review removes both the billable hours
    that funded junior associate salaries and the apprenticeship those hours
    were quietly providing, and firms have not yet built a replacement for
    the second one.
  functional: >-
    A document's relevance sometimes turns on context the model wasn't given
    --- an internal joke that reads as a threat out of context, a term that
    means something different in this specific deal than in the training
    data. A reviewing associate builds a working theory of the case while
    reading, and re-flags earlier documents when that theory changes; a
    scoring pass doesn't revise its own earlier judgments when it learns
    something new later in the same set.
rollbackPlan: >-
  A firm can staff a review the old way for any matter that calls for it, and
  the tools don't force anyone's hand. But the junior associates who would
  have learned a practice area through years of review work are the same
  people a firm would need to supervise the model's output, so
  under-staffing that apprenticeship now is a cost that arrives in about five
  years, not immediately.
---

Last notice before the break, and the last one in the clean, confident,
early-arc register: a real efficiency gain, a rollback plan that's genuinely
just inconvenient rather than impossible. Read it that way on purpose, so
