> Pick an industry someone is genuinely pitching an AI system to replace, and
> write its deprecation notice --- the same four parts this course has used
> all semester, in your own words, about a case you chose.

This is the first time you're on the writing side of the seam instead of the
reading side. The habit from the Weekly Audit Log runs in reverse here: you
already know what a reader will check for, because you've spent six weeks
checking it yourself.

## What you submit

One notice, in the same four-part shape as every lecture this semester:
sunset rationale and migration path (the pitch, in its own confident voice),
breaking changes (economic and functional, always plain), and a rollback
plan. 600–900 words total. A short note (2–3 sentences) naming your source
for the industry you chose --- a real pitch you've seen, heard, or read about
--- so a marker can check your breaking-changes section against something
real.

Due the Monday directly after the mid-semester break, so bring an industry
idea into week 6's session and use the break to find your source.
