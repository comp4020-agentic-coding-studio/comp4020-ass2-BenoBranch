# Policies and support

## How to read a lecture

Every lecture on this site is written in two registers, on purpose, every
week, with no exceptions.

The pitch and migration path are written the way the AI-consultancy pitch
they describe was actually sold: confident, persuasive, and light on what it
costs. The breaking-changes section is never written that way. It's the
plain half of the page --- checked by the teaching team, not generated
alongside the pitch, and it's where you'll find the economic and functional
cost the pitch left out. The rollback plan says, as honestly as the industry
allows, what reversing the migration would actually take.

If a lecture page reads as overconfident, that's the point up to the seam
where "Breaking changes" starts. Past that seam, it's meant to read as
plain fact-checking, not satire. Telling the two apart --- on the page, and
eventually in your own writing --- is the skill this course is teaching.

## Late work and extensions

Work handed in late loses 5% of the available mark per day, to a maximum of
five days, after which it is marked as submitted but capped at a pass. If
something outside your control is going to make a deadline genuinely
unreachable, ask for an extension before the deadline, not after it --- a
short email naming the assessment and the new date you're asking for is
enough to start that conversation.

## Declaring AI use

This course is about auditing AI-generated pitches, so it would be a strange
place to pretend AI tools don't exist. Using one is fine. What matters is
that you can defend, in your own words, every claim in the work you submit
--- if a tool wrote a sentence you can't explain or wouldn't stand behind
under questioning, that sentence shouldn't be in your submission. A short
note on what you used and for what, attached to the assessment, is all the
declaration this course asks for.

## Getting help

Session tutors are the fastest route for a question about that week's
notice or that week's marking. For anything about the course as a whole ---
its shape, its assessment weights, a clash with another commitment --- go to
the convenor. Both are reachable through the [people page](/people/).
